// projects

Key Projects & Research

Flagship · PM & Techno-Economics

HyGreen Algeria — Hybrid Solar-Wind SOEC System for Green Hydrogen & Ammonia

Led techno-economic feasibility and PM planning for a pilot-scale green ammonia plant: 8 MWp PV + 2 MW wind + 1 MW Sunfire SOEC (82.3% efficiency) with a Casale 1.5 TPD Haber-Bosch loop and waste-heat recovery (≥68% system efficiency).

  • LCOH ≤340 DZD/kg · LCOA ≤61,000 DZD/ton — 25% below fossil ammonia
  • CAPEX/OPEX modeling, supplier selection, 18-month EPCM framework for Sonatrach's Renewable Energy Division
  • LCA: ≥7,500 t/yr CO₂ avoidance (95.6% vs. grey) · 22 KPIs across 5 pillars
Energy Transition & Policy

Decarbonizing the Algerian Cement Industry

Designed a CBAM-compliant decarbonization roadmap for Algeria's cement sector (41 Mt/yr, 26.69 Mt CO₂/yr) targeting <300 kg CO₂/t by 2040 and >70% renewable share.

  • Three scenarios modeled (150B–1.5T DZD)
  • Recommended hybrid Green Hydrogen + CCUS strategy (80% capture, 346.98B DZD/yr)
  • Aligned with EU CBAM and Algeria's Hydrogen Roadmap (40 TWh by 2040)
Process Simulation

Green Ammonia via Water Electrolysis — Aspen Plus

Simulated a full green ammonia process coupling a water electrolyzer, an air separation unit (N₂), and a Haber-Bosch synthesis loop; modeled mass/energy balances and separation/recycle streams.

Embedded AI · Health

Smartwatch for Healthcare Assistance with AI

Wearable ESP32 health-monitoring smartwatch tracking heart rate, body temperature, and SpO₂ with AI-based real-time anomaly detection.

Embedded IoT · Bachelor
